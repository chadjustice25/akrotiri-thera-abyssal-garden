About, sources and licences

Thera (Santorini) is the island's later name and Akrotiri a modern village's: the Bronze Age names of the island and of the town are not known, and every building, street and square is called by a modern excavation label. The three layers: Found shows only what was dug up, where it was found, with the land and those buildings whole and in one plain tone, because no colour is sourced and the height each wall survives to is not published; Inferred adds what the evidence points to, and Imagined, which will add what brings the place to life, holds nothing yet. A change of layer spreads outward from the excavated centre as a way of showing: it is not the order in which the site was dug. The date is a seasonal one: a day counted from the spring equinox and named as today's calendar names that time of year, not a date in any calendar of the time; only the sun follows it. The town: its centre follows the excavated plan. Everything beyond the edge of the excavated walls (key L, or the switch above) is reconstruction by rule, to about 10 ha in all, and no colour of any wall, roof or paving is sourced. The town is shown from outside and empty: people, animals, plants and interiors are still to come, and no harbour is built, because none has been found.
