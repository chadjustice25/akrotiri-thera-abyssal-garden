Thera (Santorini) is the island's later name and Akrotiri a modern village's: the Bronze Age names of the island and of the town are not known, and every building, street and square is called by a modern excavation label. The town: its centre follows the excavated plan. Everything beyond the edge of the excavated walls (key L, or the switch above) is reconstruction by rule, to about 10 ha in all, and no colour of any wall, roof or paving is sourced. The town is shown from outside and empty: people, animals, plants and interiors are still to come, and no harbour is built, because none has been found.
