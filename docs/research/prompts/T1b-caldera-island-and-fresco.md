## Topics
A. **Who reads the Ship Procession fresco as a picture of Thera's caldera, and exactly what they argue.** Start with Karátson et al. 2018 (*Scientific Reports* 8: 7026, open access) and Karátson et al. 2020 (*Journal of Volcanology and Geothermal Research*, DOI 10.1016/j.jvolgeores.2020.106911). Then find the other archaeologists and geologists who make or test the argument (for example W. L. Friedrich, F. McCoy, C. Doumas, L. Morgan, A. Vlachopoulos, S. Marinatos). Identify which parts of the frieze each one maps to which real places:
   - the town on the left inside a ring of water;
   - the town on the right with its harbour;
   - the coloured rocks and the hills;
   - the water channel.
B. **The case against, or for other places.** Readings that put the departure town or the arrival town elsewhere (Crete, Libya, another Cycladic island), and the art historians' view that the frieze is a composed or idealised scene, not a map.
C. **The island in the caldera ("Pre-Kameni", a modern label).** Its height, footprint, shape, rock and colour, and any evidence of buildings or use on it. Grade each figure. Say clearly where the only evidence for a feature is the fresco.
D. **T1's open numbers:** the depth of the old caldera bay, the width and depth of its northern channel, where the harbour of Akrotiri may have been, and the height of Profitis Ilias. Use bathymetry, drilling and seismic studies of the caldera floor, and studies of the pre-eruption landscape.
E. **What the fresco shows, as a description for the builder** (grade B: "shown is not present"). Look at the local image `akrotiri-frescoes-sources/ship-procession-fresco-akrotiri-749.jpg`. It is a reference only: it stays out of git (D6) and nothing from it is copied into the build. Describe the landforms, the rock colours, the vegetation, the water, the buildings (storeys, windows, materials) and the harbour. Each feature must be tied to a published description of the fresco wherever one exists.

- **T1b-3:** The town on the right is Akrotiri.
