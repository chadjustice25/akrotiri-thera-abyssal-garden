- **The monkeys are handled correctly.** No row grades a monkey above B; the absence statement (#55) is Georma's, accurately reported; both identifications are presented as a live debate. The langur proposal (Pareja et al.) and the vervet replies (Urbani and Youlatos; Pruetz and Greenlaw) argue over **one painting, the Monkeys fresco of Building Beta room B6**. No source opened identifies the species of the Xeste 3 or Porter's Lodge monkeys. Georma's own *Cercopithecus aethiops* identification cites an encyclopaedia entry; the specialist identification behind it is Marinatos's *C. callitrichus* (= *C. sabaeus*, the West African green monkey). In current taxonomy all of these sit in *Chlorocebus*.
- **No species is graded A on art alone.** #26 grades the murex pigment A (it was analysed), not the crocus. #63 grades the gold figurine A as an object and its species B. #65 grades the painted fish B.
- **Usable to populate the world: yes, with the corrections below.** The grade-A core (crops, fuel and wild shrubs from the kitchen, the buried olive tree and the Therasia shrub, the kitchen bones and fish, murex) matches its sources number for number. The painted fauna and flora are correctly kept at B. Topic E (sizes, colours, group sizes, seasons for 15–20 species) is thin, as the report says.
