Grades follow the brief's modern-excavation reading (A: the excavators' report of that season or a dated plan they published; B: a later account by a team member, a peer-reviewed study, or excavators' accounts that disagree; C: second-hand or inference). Quotations are short. Greek is quoted with a translation.

One request was refused and I did not work around it: the Society's map data file (`admin/components/lemmas/geojson.php`).

## 3. Corrections

Apply these before any year enters the data.

1. **Xeste 3 begun 1969, not 1971 or 1973** (Section 1, building table, #59, #67).
   - The first ashlar stones and rosette fresco fragments appeared beside pillar 4 in 1969 (ΠΑΕ 1969 p. 173).
   - The 1994 plan puts pillar 4 at Xeste 3's north-east corner.
   - Έργον 1993 speaks of Xeste 3's relief fresco of the Rosettes (Room 9).
   - Grade B.
2. **Xeste E = Xeste 3: supported** (#59). Regrade C to B and change its source.
   - The 1971 building was found "κυρίως παρὰ τὸν πεσσὸν 4" (mainly beside pillar 4), west of Γ along pillars 1–4 (ΠΑΕ 1971 p. 201).
   - The 1994 plan shows pillar 3 on Γ's west side and pillar 4 at Xeste 3's north-east corner.
   - Έργον 1973, the source #59 cites, gives no location.
3. **East Court = Double Horns Square: supported** (#61). Regrade C to A.
   - Shaft 35 lay in the East Court (ΠΑΕ 1971 p. 183).
   - The 1994 plan prints pillar 35 inside «Πλατεία Διπλῶν Κεράτων».
   - The 1971 plan's east–west wall south of shaft 35 matches Xeste 2's north wall.
4. **Cenotaph Square begun 1967, not 1969** (building table, #94, #146).
   - Bronos 1a was dug outside the north wall that fronts the square (ΠΑΕ 1967 p. 139; ΠΑΕ 1970 pp. 161–2).
   - The 1969 pyre date is right (A).
5. **Bronos 1a met Complex Delta's north front: supported** (#146). Regrade C to B.
   - ΠΑΕ 1970 itself ties the court façade to the 1967 trench (footnote to ΠΑΕ 1967 pp. 139–40, fig. 4).
   - The antechamber Δ4 is the North unit's (Doumas 2016 p. 61; ΠΑΕ 1970 p. 162).
   - The 1994 plan puts pillars 16 and 17 (dug in that court in 1970) in «Πλατεία Κενοταφίου».
   - Bronos 1 and 1a are the same building (ΠΑΕ 1969 p. 159), so Complex Delta North dates to **25 May 1967**.
6. **The 1969 court = Millhouse Square: supported** (#147). Regrade C to B.
   - The 1971 plan labels «ΒΟΡΕΙΑ ΑΥΛΗ» directly south of Δ15.
   - The 1994 plan labels the same spot «ΠΛΑΤΕΙΑ ΜΥΛΩΝΟΣ».
7. **Dactyls Street begun 1971, not 1977** (building table, #76–#78). Regrade #78 C to B.

The 1967 work ran in three spells: until 30 May, then 20–26 June, then 12–31 July. Excavation resumed on 21 June (both dates in ΠΑΕ 1967 p. 135–6, text).

**How the areas were joined:**
- **1968.** Regular work kept "εἰς τὰς δύο κυρίας περιοχὰς" (to the two main areas) of 1967: Arvanitis 1 to the north and Bronos 2 to the south (ΠΑΕ 1968 p. 89). The storeroom got shelter no. I (Έργον 1968 fig. 90).
- **1969.**
  - A 32 × 27 m shelter (about 750 m²) was built over Β–Γ (ΠΑΕ 1969 p. 148).
  - The unexcavated middle (ex-Bronos 1–1a) was called the «γέφυρα» (bridge) (p. 150).
  - The 1969 general plan shows three separate patches: Α to the north; the bridge pieces; Β–Γ to the south.
- **1970.** The aim was to join "τοῦ βορειοτάτου καὶ τοῦ νοτιωτάτου σημείου" (the northernmost and the southernmost points) (Έργον 1970). The bridge was named Sector Δ on starting and proved to join Β to the south (ΠΑΕ 1970 p. 161). Shelter over Δ: 700–800 m² (§3, item 15).
- **1971.** Shafts 22–36 carried the roof over "ὁ τέως ἐλεύθερος χῶρος μεταξὺ τῶν τομέων Α καὶ Δ" (the formerly open space between Sectors Α and Δ) (ΠΑΕ 1971 p. 181). The join was by roof. The ground between was dug from 1977.

## 10. Shelters, closure, and who directs today

- **Shelters.**
  - Zinc sheets over the storeroom in 1967 (P67).
  - Dexion with Hellenit sheets: shelter no. I over Α in 1968; 32 × 27 m (about 750 m²) over Β–Γ in 1969; 700–800 m² over Δ in 1970; the roof extended over the Α–Δ gap in 1971 on shafts 22–36.
  - The new shelter's shafts were dug in 1999–2001: 3 × 4 m, 15–20 m deep; 35 investigated by the end of 2000 (E00); 91 in total by Doumas 2016; "over 100" in FD14.
  - Construction ran from 2002; E05 shows the north sector "with the shelter completed".
- **Collapse:** 23 September 2005, part of the new shelter; one visitor killed (E06, E07). Confirmed.
- **Reopening.** The Society delivered the shelter "πλῆρες καὶ σὲ λειτουργία στὶς 12 Ἀπριλίου 2012" (complete and working on 12 April 2012) (E19, page image).
  - *Archaeology* (dated 12 April 2012) says it "reopened to tourists today".
  - The 11 April press reports were not opened by me.
  - Use **12 April 2012** for the delivery (A). Keep 11–12 April for the opening to visitors (C).
- **Who directs today: not settled.**
  - The Society's own latest report naming a director is Έργον 2020 (Doumas, through K. Birtacha).
  - The Ephorate of the Cyclades' site page (undated, © 2026) says the excavation "continues to this day with Professor Christos Doumas, under the auspices of the Athens Archaeological Society". That is grade C and undated.
  - Two plain web searches found no official dated statement after 2020.
