## Corrections to apply before building

1. **#37, Section 3 and Summary:** the ~25 m at Mavrorachidi is depth to andesitic bedrock at a coastal core, not a pre-eruption valley floor. Do not use it as a strip-off depth without the core log.
2. **#24, Summary bullet 7 and the "~32 m" INFERENCE row:** the 2/5/25 m averages are not stated to be on-land averages (they apportion intra-caldera infill in Karátson 2018). Keep the ~32 m only as an INFERENCE and record any strip-off depth as a liberty (§6) until isopach maps (Druitt et al. 1999; Bond and Sparks 1976) are checked.
3. **#33:** the eruption followed a second, undated earthquake, not the mid-17th-century one.
4. **#6:** reword H&M as one Minoan collapse of about 19 km³, foundering in stages, overlapping an older caldera (c. 100 ka tephra in their abstract).
5. **#45:** grade A for the EBA–MC occupation only; "abandoned before LC I" is grade B.
6. **#38 and Summary bullet 8:** 18 listed places, not about 16; Koloumbos is a few grave sherds, not a single vessel.
7. **#31:** add that "formed today's caldera walls" is a press simplification at odds with #4 and #19.
8. **#28:** 4.85 ± 0.7 km³ is DRE; the 3.2 km³ (#29) is a different measure. Do not average them.
9. **#25:** name the calibration curve (standard §2) in W1, not here; the date stays out of the world until `docs/WORLD_CLOCK.md` exists.
10. **#50:** drop "1958–2001" (not in the abstract).
11. **#53:** use `COPERNICUS/DEM/GLO30_2024_1` and record its licence; note GLO-30 is a surface model (buildings, vegetation) and needs care at the Fira/Oia cliff towns.
12. **#54:** record EMODnet's licence from its terms page (CC BY 4.0 by default, per a search result) before use.
13. **Sources §14:** correct the Mavromati thesis title.
14. **#34:** label 1.2 ha as the excavated area, not the town's size.
15. The lane's landscape reading of grades A/B/C (report lines 9–12) should become a decision (amending the standard's §3 for landscape), since §3 was written for objects.

## Gaps, and whether a follow-up lane is worth running

Not found in any source opened, by the report or by this check:
- **Depth of the pre-Minoan bay**, and the **width and depth of any northern opening.** Lead: Druitt and Francaviglia 1992; Athanassas et al. 2016 (Bull. Volc.); IODP 398 core reports; Eriksen et al. 1990 (stromatolite elevations).
- **Pre-Kameni's height and footprint.** Only volumes exist; any shape is a liberty.
- **Isopach (thickness) maps of the Minoan units on land**, especially on the Akrotiri peninsula and at the town itself (Druitt et al. 1999, Memoir 19; Bond and Sparks 1976). This is the biggest gap for terrain work.
- **The pre-eruption south coastline and harbour.** Only the Theodorakopoulou abstract; the full chapter and any later coring (e.g. the Akrotiri harbour geophysics) should be read.
- **Santorini's own vertical land movement since c. 1650 BCE.** Without it the −3.6 m Cycladic value stays grade C.
- **Present-day climate normals for Thira** (HNMS was unreachable) and an Aegean marine-core or speleothem record that is published, not a rejected preprint (Finné et al. 2011, 2019; Triantaphyllou et al. 2009).
- **Profitis Ilias summit height and its limestone cap**: sample from the DEM; the cap needs a geology source.
- **Colours** of pumice, soils, basement and limestone: none sourced.

**A short follow-up lane is worth running**, scoped to five items that block the terrain build: (1) Minoan deposit isopachs on land, (2) Druitt and Francaviglia 1992 and Athanassas et al. 2016 full texts for the pre-Minoan caldera outline and bay depth, (3) the Akrotiri harbour coring results in full, (4) a Santorini-specific relative sea-level or uplift marker, and (5) the terms of use of the current Copernicus and EMODnet products. Everything else in T1 is usable now with the corrections above, provided the unknowns go into the liberties table.
