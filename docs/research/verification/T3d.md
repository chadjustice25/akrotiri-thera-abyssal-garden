## Verdict

- **Rows checked: 153 of 153** (59 claim numbers; the report repeats a claim on one row per source). Every row was assessed against its cited URL. **Confirmed: 63. Partly: 29. Not supported: 4** (rows of claims 29 [21], 39 [17], 40 [41], 59 [62]). **Could not check: 57**, all because the source would not open (ResearchGate, dokumen.pub, KU ScholarWorks, Newcastle theses, SciSpace, Taylor & Francis, OAPEN, the AJA pages, the Flora of Greece portal, the Strasser and Scribd copies) or is paywalled beyond its summary (Poursat 2022, Anderson 2024, Papageorgiou 2016). Rows confirmed against an abstract or a museum record are confirmed against that text only.
- **Coverage.** All 47 grade-A rows were attempted: 23 could be checked (12 confirmed, 11 partly) and 24 could not. All 42 rows of Section 3, all six verdicts of Section 4 and all 12 Summary bullets were checked (tables below). All 70 URLs in Section 7 and Works cited were requested.
- **Sources: 40 of 70 URLs open** and show the work cited. A 41st (Sotiropoulou et al. 2021, [29]) refused the cited page, but the same article opened from MDPI's file server. 29 would not open. **No fabricated source, quotation or number was found.** Every source that opened is real and matches its title, though some carry wrong dates or no author (see Sources check). There is one clear misreading of a number: the palm's "20–40 fronds" is Morgan's figure for a real date palm, not the painting. Many numbers stay unverified because their sources are blocked: the room heights and sizes of Xeste 3, Delta 2 and West House Room 5, the pigment percentages, the 37,000-year palm fossil and the "five lilies".
- **Citation markers.** 151 of 153 row markers point at the URL in their row. Row 152 (claim 58) is marked [66] but its URL is [67]. Row 153 (claim 59) is marked [13] (the Xeste 3 chapter) but its URL is [62] (the Greek checklist). The bracketed tags [T13], [T15], [T32A], [T38] and [T39] are Heraklion Museum inventory numbers (Greek Τ). Each points at the right museum record. They could be mistaken for this project's T-lane claim references, so write them as "HM Τ15" and so on.
- **The grade-A plant core holds, but the charcoal list needs care.** Barley, wheat, pulses (above all *Lathyrus clymenum*), olive, almond, pear, *Pistacia*, juniper, pine and tamarisk are physically attested at Akrotiri. The report goes further than its sources in three places:
  - **Oak** is not in Bottema-Mac Gillavry's West House samples at all.
  - **Chestnut** may have been imported.
  - **Alder**'s stream is Bottema's conditional reading, and her alder came only from outside the West House, in layers that may predate the final phase.
  - **Fig and grape** have seeds at Akrotiri (T3 #1, #4), but no fig or vine wood had been found anywhere there (Bottema 2004).
- **Shown is not present is handled well.** No row grades a painted plant above B for Thera; Cretan, Kean and Melian paintings are C; pigments are A as pigments only. The weak spots are claims that a painting element is an object fact (claim 26, techniques from Kea) and A grades on rows whose cited source covers only the architecture (claims 4, 11, 14, 18, 19).
- **No ancient word is invented.** Linear A is correctly called undeciphered. The report does use later Greek labels without marking them as modern conventions: *Potnia Theron*, *ikria*, *asaminthos* and *kymbe*.
- **Usable for building plants: yes, with the corrections below.** Use it to decide what stays inside paintings and on pots, and what may grow outdoors. Do not use its Section 3 room and frieze sizes until Palyvou 2005, Strasser 2001 or Warren 1979 are read; none of them opened.

**The palm fossil** (*Phoenix theophrasti*, about 37,000 years ago, Fira palaeosol). The cited source, Gros-Balthazard et al. 2021 [64], would not open. Two opened sources date Santorini's fossil palms differently. Bottema, citing Velitzelos 1990, gives *P. theophrasti* remains from about 50,000 years ago. Doumas gives plant inclusions in lavas 50,000–60,000 years old that include olive and "various species of palm". Either way the fossil is about 35,000–58,000 years before 1615 BCE: **grade C**, and it says nothing about palms on Bronze Age Thera. The Greek checklist (Englera 31, [62]) records *P. theophrasti* on Crete and with "?" for the Cyclades. The Amorgos locality could not be checked.

## Corrections to apply before building

1. **Charcoal and the outdoor woodland (Summary 2, claims 44, 49, 50, Game hooks).**
   - Oak is not in Bottema's West House samples. Cite oak from Asouti (phases not split) and the LC IA kitchen (deciduous oak, T3 #6).

   - Alder, *Pistacia* and pear came only from outside the West House, in deposits that may be Middle Cycladic or early LC I.

   - Pine and juniper may have been imported (Bottema), though their use as fuel (T3 #6) suggests local trees.
   - Do not build "open oak woodland" or an alder stream as grade A.
