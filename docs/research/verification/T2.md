The published plans are copyright (Doumas 2016), the TiMMA data need the director's agreement, and the 2013 laser scan is not public. Laying out the streets is therefore the largest liberty.

## 3. Corrections

Apply these before any number enters the data.

1. **Outer wall thickness (Numbers table and Summary):** the report gives 0.60–0.75 m from the threshold (0.60), the partition (0.65) and the door record (0.75), but leaves out the entrance-window record (Str1016), whose "width" is 0.80. If TiMMA's "width" is the depth through the wall, as the report assumes, the range is **0.60–0.80 m**. TiMMA labels none of the axes and gives no units, and the threshold's 0.60 is the width of a block, not necessarily of the wall. Enter it as 0.60–0.80 m, ground and first floor of the West House only, [verify axes], until Palyvou 1999, 2005 or 2019 is read.
2. **"Street raised above old ground floors: about 1 m" (Numbers; Summary bullet 3; Game hooks):** the only direct evidence is Delta 16 (#56).
