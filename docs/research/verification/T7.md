Every row was checked by re-opening its cited source during this run. HTML pages were read with WebFetch and, where a passage had to be found exactly, also with `curl`. ## Verdict

- **Claims checked: 100 of 100.** That covers every grade-A row, every row a number in "Numbers for the builder" rests on, the every-fifth sample, and the Summary, the assumptions and the debates.
- **Results:**
  - **Confirmed:** 94.
  - **Partly:** 6 (#5, #6, #18, #21, #30, #95).
  - **Not supported:** 0. **Could not check:** 0.
  - Rows that rest only on an abstract or a database record (#1–#3, #35, #68, #77, #78, #84, #91, #92) are confirmed against that abstract or record, not against the full paper. The report says so itself.
- **Numbers:** every number in the Claims table and in Section 3 matches its source, including the plume heights (8.5 ± 1.5, 11.6 ± 2.1, 14.3 ± 1.1, 18.0 ± 1.3, 28.4 ± 1.0 and 21.7 ± 1.1 km), the 36 ± 5 km older figure, the mass eruption rates and every Karstens 2023 volume. Three numbers need care:
  - The Ministry's "a few score of days" translates a Greek phrase meaning "a few tens of days", so the INFERENCE of 40–100 days should read roughly 20–90 days (#6).
  - Bruins gives the Palaikastro source wave as +35 to −15 m, while Nomikou cites the same model as +35 or −11 m. The report follows Bruins correctly; the difference is the sources'.
  - McCoy's "3+ cm/hr" is inconsistent with his own "3 cm/min" (#54, which the report already flags). Only cm/min fits a fall several metres thick laid down in several hours (verifier INFERENCE).
- **Sources: 26 of 26 URLs open, and each is the work cited.** The authors, titles, journals, years and DOIs match. **No sign of fabricated sources, quotations or numbers.** The "Sources not opened" list was spot-checked: Ntokos 2025 exists as described, with a CC BY licence in its Crossref record.
- **Grades:** all are defensible under `docs/HISTORICAL_STANDARD.md` §3 and the lane's own reading of it (report lines 9–13).
  - #63 is graded C as a model. By the lane's rule it could be B (a model built on Theran bathymetry). Either is acceptable.
  - #11 and #13 are grade A at second hand, through Rutter's teaching text; the X1 verification treated the same rows the same way.
- **The one substantive problem is the timing of the last months (Summary 4, Section 3 rows 1–3, Debate 1).** The report lists "a few score of days", "a few months" and "two or three decades" as rival estimates of one interval. They are probably not. The sources point to two intervals, after two different earthquakes. Marthari's decades most likely measure the first earthquake to the eruption; the Ministry's tens of days measure the second. See Corrections 1–3.
- **Usable: yes, with the corrections below.**
  - The eruption half of the lane (phases, plume heights, volumes, dispersal, tsunamis, flooding) matches its sources figure for figure and can be built from.
  - The abandonment evidence (no bodies, little gold or silver, doors shut, bronze and pots left behind) is confirmed. It holds only for the roughly 10% of the town that has been dug (#32).
  - The end-of-town timeline is usable once the two earthquakes are kept apart.

## Corrections to apply before building

1. **Keep the two earthquakes and their two intervals apart (Summary 4, Section 3 rows 1–3, Section 4 T7-1, Debate 1).**
   - *First earthquake.* The Ministry puts a seismic destruction at the start of Late Cycladic I, about the mid-17th century BC, followed at once by rebuilding (#4). Rutter dates "the great earthquake" quite early in LM IA, ca. 1650 or ca. 1560 BC. He dates the burial to ca. 1625 or ca. 1550/1540 BC (X1 #28), which puts one or more decades between them on his own figures. McCoy names Marthari's Seismic Destruction Level as an earthquake at the end of MM IIIB, distinct from the quake just before the eruption (#46).
   - *Marthari's interval.* Her pottery comparison (#16), "years, perhaps two or three decades", is therefore most likely the interval from this **first** earthquake to the eruption, not from the last one. This is verifier INFERENCE: Marthari's own papers were not opened by the report or by this check.
   - *Second earthquake.* For the **second** earthquake, the sources that speak of it give a short interval:
     - the Ministry: "a few tens of days" (#6);
     - Michailidou: clearing "during a short interval" (p. 137);
     - Doumas 2019: the eruption came soon after repairs began (#31);
     - McCoy: a large quake "immediately" before phase 1 (#46).
   - *Doumas 1983.* His "a few months" (#16) cannot be placed with certainty on either earthquake from what was opened.
   - *What to write.* Rewrite Summary 4 and the Section 3 rows to name which earthquake each interval starts from, and keep Rutter's one-earthquake reading as the alternative view.
2. **Say which evidence belongs to which earthquake, where the sources do.**
   - *The final interval.* Michailidou ties the walled-up doors, new walls, furniture moved outside, demolition hammers and unfinished street clearing to the interval after the earthquake just before the eruption (#9, #10, #12). The half-plastered West House room (#13) was being decorated at the moment of abandonment, so it belongs there too.
   - *The first rebuilding.* The Ministry's caption for Telchines Street ties the raised street level, made of demolition rubble that was not reused, to the reconstruction after the earthquake. That fits the first earthquake's rebuilding (#11).
   - *The conflict.* Rutter instead treats the rubble walls in the streets and the demolition balls as part of one clearing after one earthquake. Both readings must be kept until an excavation report settles it.

## Gaps

- **Marthari 1984 and 1990** (the Seismic Destruction Level and the pottery interval) and **Doumas 1983** are the primary sources for the interval question. Neither was opened. They are the most important follow-up for this lane.
- **Heiken and McCoy 1990** (the precursor gap) and **Druitt 2014** (the vent history of phases 0–4) are still unread. The HAL copy of Druitt 2014 was behind a bot check for the report's agent; it is worth another try.
- **Ntokos 2025** is open access (CC BY) and argues for a May eruption. It is unread, and the only new seasonal evidence.
- **The excavators' own reports** (Praktika, Doumas's annual reports) are needed to assign each clearing feature to the first or the second earthquake (Correction 2).
- **The magnitude-7 reading** rests on Vougioukalakis 2006, which no one has opened.
- **The Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program's VEI** for the eruption was not seen, so the VEI 6 reading remains the report's INFERENCE.
- **Rhodes** (Trianda tephra) and **the Nile Delta shards** are ungraded.
- **The time of day** is not known from any source.
- **Where the people went** is not known from any source: Rutter and Cioni say off the island, Doumas says caught on it. No refugee evidence exists in any source opened.
