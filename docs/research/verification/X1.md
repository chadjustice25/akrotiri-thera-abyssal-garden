## 3. Corrections to apply (in `WORLD_CLOCK.md`, or in the report if it is revised)

- **C1 (#16, Section 3):** the 1545–1504 cal BCE (48.9%) date is the weighted mean of three Ahmose-mudbrick samples; the straw alone is 1542–1427 cal BCE (66.4%).
- **C2 (#62, Sections 1 and 3):** Manning 2024's abstract gives c. 1611 BCE as "more likely" and c. 1561 BCE as the alternative. State both.
- **C3 (#13, Section 1 item 1):** Pearson 2023 puts the most probability in the later 16th century and marginalises the 1611 marker; add the offset-corrected Therasia range c. 1602–1502 BCE.
- **C4 (#19, Section 8):** the 1628 Greenland tephra was already rejected as Theran by 2008; only the 1645 ± 7 candidate was doubted.
- **C5 (#58, Section 6):** remove the claim that a workshop dates the paintings' making; the paintings belong to phase C/3 (c. 1479–1450 BC).
- **C6 (Section 1 item 9, #51):** the northern Levant destructions are not all "linked to Hattusili I": Herrmann et al. report that Ebla's excavator now attributes Ebla's destruction to Pizikarra of Nineveh. Also, the 1632–1610 BC (56.2%) range is Zincirli's alone.
- **C7 (Section 1 item 7; assumption X1-6):** under UEE/Schneider, Dyn 13 ends 1656 and Dyn 15 begins 1640, so at c. 1650 the 15th Dynasty had not yet begun. X1-6 is "partly confirmed" for 1650 and confirmed for 1640–1532.
- **C8 (#70, Sections 5 and 6):** kerma.ch's "c. 1580 BC" for the Hyksos alliance proposal does not fit UEE/Schneider, which places Kamose (whose stela records the intercepted letter) at 1555–1548. The world clock must use one Egyptian chronology; either re-date the event on UEE or mark the kerma.ch figure as a different chronology.
- **C9 (#91):** the Tell el-Dab'a ranges are phase boundaries, not stratum ranges.
- **C10 (Section 4 note and X1-4):** "LM IA lasted at least 75 years in both tables" is wrong. The high table (1700/1675–1625/1600) allows 50–100 years; the low table gives 120. Write "50 to 120 years, depending on chronology".
- **C11 (Section 6, pumice row):** "15 or more sites in Egypt, the Levant, Cyprus, Anatolia and the Aegean" is not in any cited source; #20 speaks of over 400 samples from Egypt and the Levant. Use that.
