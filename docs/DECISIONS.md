# Decisions

Numbered decisions for this project. A decision is changed only by a newer one that names it. Open questions are listed at the end until they are decided. Only Chad's session edits this file (`AGENTS.md` §3).

| # | Decision | Who, when | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Fork Noniv/abyssal-garden as `chadjustice25/akrotiri-thera-abyssal-garden`.** `origin` is the fork and `upstream` is Noniv's repo. Upstream's MIT `LICENSE`, with its copyright line, stays as it is, and the README credits upstream. The scene stays upstream's until the first Thera work order replaces it. | Chad 2026-11-15 | A working procedural WebGPU world (terrain, water, light, plants, animals, sound) in one file is the fastest start. A fork of a public repo is public on GitHub. |
| D2 | **Scope: Thera is the proof of concept.** Akrotiri and the island as they stood just before the Late Bronze Age eruption. Later the same world expands to other places at the same moment (Egypt, Byblos, Crete and more). | Chad 2026-11-15 | Thera is the best-preserved Bronze Age town in the Aegean, and its date problem forces the cross-site clock to be settled first. |
| D3 | **The moment: c. 1650 BCE, give or take 100–150 years (1800–1500 BCE), with one world clock for every place** (`docs/HISTORICAL_STANDARD.md` §1–2). Thera's exact anchor year is open (O1). | Chad 2026-11-15 | Places added later must agree with Thera on what year it is. The eruption date itself is disputed between about 1630 and 1500 BCE (to be checked by W1). |
| D4 | **Research runs on Gemini 4 Argon Deep Research, one lane per chat, and is checked by Claude before use.** Prompts are in `docs/research/prompts/` and reports in `docs/research/argon/`; checks go to `docs/research/verification/`. The grades and report format follow Beyond the Gates of Uruk's historian briefs and unghost's research lanes. | Chad 2026-11-15 | Long cited reports at no cost to Claude usage. Both earlier projects found that reports carry errors a check against the sources catches. |
| D5 | **The project uses the unghost harness as it stands:** `.harness/project.json`, work orders in `docs/tasks/`, and `checks/build.sh` as the gate. It sits outside unghost's D97/D107 pilot order, and it asks for no new harness machinery. | Chad 2026-11-15 ("I know this project isn't in the plan and that is OK") | The work-order, gate and log discipline costs little to adopt now, and it keeps the history comparable with the pilots. |
| D6 | **Reference images (`akrotiri-frescoes-sources/`) stay out of git.** | Session 1 default, 2026-11-15; confirmed by Chad 2026-11-18 ("D6 confirmed for image rights") after the T6 rights research (`research/verification/T6.md`): a faithful image of a state-owned antiquity needs a licence if it is spread for profit (Greek Law 4858/2021, Art. 46), modern photographs carry their own copyright, and re-creating a motif in code falls outside the licence rule (research, not legal advice) | The fork is public. Reproducing images of Greek antiquities is regulated, and photographs carry their own copyright. Research lane T6 (topic F) checks both. |
| D7 | **Gate `build`:** `checks/build.sh` passes only if `source/` builds, the committed `index.html` is that build byte for byte, nothing is loaded at run time, and the page is within a size budget. The budget is **256 kB, provisional**; upstream's page is 98 kB. | Session 1, 2026-11-15 | Upstream's `index.html` rebuilds byte-identically (checked 2026-11-15, Node 22.23, esbuild 0.28.2), so drift between the source and the page is detectable. The control cases (stale page, over budget, runtime script, missing esbuild) each fail for their own reason. |
| D8 | **Lane X1 tests whether Argon now finishes long prompts and reports.** X1 (`prompts/X1-world-clock-full.md`, a 9.7 KB prompt asking for 12 sections) runs first, as round 0. If it finishes with every section and well over 60 claim rows, it replaces W1 and W2, and the roughly 7 KB prompt limit in `LESSONS.md` is lifted for later lanes. If it stalls, W1 and W2 run as planned and the limit stands. | Chad 2026-11-15 ("Approved.") | Google appears to have fixed the bug behind the October stalls (Chad, 2026-11-15). One long, consistent world-clock report is better than two halves that must be reconciled, and W1 and W2 stay ready as the fallback. |
| D9 | **While Chad is away from the Mac, research runs through two private artifacts.** The **Thera Research Desk** (<https://claude.ai/artifact/9pEpfRsWWDtP7sKjV4NQoq>) copies the prompts, tracks lane status, and stores pasted reports as Markdown assets with metadata in its `reports` collection. The **Thera Project Board** (<https://claude.ai/artifact/SPwJ88U5JryHybwsjTn7js>) collects answers to the open decisions and notes for Claude. A report saved in the Desk counts as delivered: Claude reads it from there and copies it to `docs/research/argon/<lane>_Argon.md` before checking it. Answers on the Board become decisions only when Claude records them here. | Chad 2026-11-15 ("Approved.") | Chad works from an iPad for hours at a time. The Desk removes the Mac-only steps (`pbpaste`, Files to repo), and the repo stays the record. |
| D10 | **Check `views`:** `checks/views.mjs` renders the page in headless Chrome on the Mac's GPU, photographs the six preset viewpoints, and fails if the page does not start on a hardware adapter, logs an error, loads anything at run time, or gives a picture with no content. The pictures go to `.harness/views/` (ignored by git), and the latest run is published to the **Thera Views** artifact (<https://claude.ai/artifact/EAM2iAgZHr4toJ39qt9C9L>). It runs alongside the `build` gate (D7), not inside it, because it needs this Mac's Chrome and GPU. The detail level is fixed at High unless `--level` says otherwise, and the page's own resolution scaler stays on, so the stats line under each picture shows the resolution it settled at. | Session 1, 2026-11-16, built after Chad asked for the research (for Chad to confirm) | Terrain and town work will be judged by eye, and Chad reviews from an iPad. First run: Chrome 157 headless used the Apple `metal-3` adapter (no software fallback), the world built in 3.3 s, and all six views rendered. The control cases (a page that fails to start, a page that loads a file at run time, Chrome missing) each FAIL or ERROR for their own reason. |
| D11 | **Keep the engine law: one self-contained `index.html`, everything procedural, no image, model or sound files** (`AGENTS.md` §5, closes O2). This holds unless the work shows that the Thera world, being much larger than upstream's scene, cannot fit it. In that case a newer decision changes the law, and says what grew too large and why. | Chad 2026-11-18 ("Yes, unless we determine it needs to change because it is a much larger project than the original source project") | Keeps upstream's strengths (one file, nothing to download, no image rights to clear) while admitting that real terrain and a whole town may outgrow them. The size budget (D7, O6) is where that pressure will show first. |
| D12 | **The viewer can swim, walk and fly** (closes O3). Each mode needs something different: swimming needs the water (the sea and the caldera), walking needs ground to stand on and the town to collide with, and flying needs a view of the whole island. The terrain work order decides which mode comes first and how the viewer switches between them. | Chad 2026-11-18 ("swim, walk, and fly") | The island is best understood from the air, the town on foot, and the harbour and the sea from the water. |
| D13 | **Research lanes run as Claude research agents in this session while Gemini is unreliable** (amends D4 and D8). Each lane runs in its own background agent, named `Research: <lane> <topic>`. The agent works from the lane's prompt in `docs/research/prompts/` (the text below the `---` line), cites only sources it actually opened, and writes its report to `docs/research/claude/<lane>_Claude.md` in the lane's report format. A separate agent, named `Verify: <lane> claims against sources`, then checks the key claims and writes `docs/research/verification/<lane>.md`; the agent that wrote a report never checks it. X1 and T1 run first as the pilot. The other lanes start only after their reports show that the instructions work. Argon can still be used for any lane when it is working again. | Chad 2026-11-18 ("Gemini is having a bug degrading sessions from Argon to Hydrogen. Let's do the research here") | Subagents can be launched, named and collected from one session, and they write to the repository directly, so nothing has to be pasted from the iPad. Each agent starts with only its lane prompt, so its context stays on one topic. Checking with a different agent keeps the check independent of the writer. Piloting two lanes first means a flaw in the instructions costs two runs, not eight. |
| D14 | **Fable 5.2 is kept for the build, and research stays on Opus 5.5.** Work orders that change what the world looks like or how it runs (terrain, water, light, the town, movement) run in their own session on Claude Fable 5.2, at effort `high`, raised to `xhigh` for the hardest shader and lighting passes when the views check shows a gain. Research and verification agents run on the session's default model. The aim is a world that is visually engrossing and smooth, at the frontier of what runs in a browser, without giving up the evidence rules. | Chad 2026-11-18 ("I'd rather put the Fable 5.2 effort into making this supremely excellent, smooth, and visually engrossing") | The extra capability pays off most on long, hard engine work. Research quality is protected by the source rules and an independent check instead. |
| D15 | **The world clock: the high (radiocarbon) Aegean chronology, and the living town** (closes O1, apart from the exact year: O7). Thera erupts c. 1611–1600 BCE (Manning 2022 and 2024, IntCal20). Egypt follows UEE/Schneider, Mesopotamia the high Middle Chronology (Babylon falls in 1595), and radiocarbon IntCal20. The late radiocarbon reading (c. 1561 BCE) and the low reading (c. 1525–1490 BC) stay recorded beside it. Thera's moment is the living town: after its rebuilding from the earthquake of c. 1650 and before the final earthquake and evacuation. This amends `HISTORICAL_STANDARD.md` §1, which said "the town's last season". The details are in `docs/WORLD_CLOCK.md`. | Chad 2026-11-18 (chose "High, c. 1610 BCE" and "The living town") | The X1 check found that the high reading is the only one whose neighbours (Hyksos Avaris, late Old Babylonian Babylon, Old Hittite Hatti) are all named in verified sources. A living town is the world worth walking through; the empty, damaged town of the last weeks can be a later moment. |
| D16 | **The world's year is 1615 BCE (high chronology)** (closes O7). Every place in the world shows 1615 BCE. At Akrotiri it is the living town at its fullest, decades after the rebuild that followed the first earthquake, and a few years before the final earthquake (about 20–90 days before the eruption of c. 1611–1600 BCE). Egypt has Dynasty 15 at Avaris and Dynasty 17 at Thebes (UEE/Schneider). Babylon is in the last decades of its First Dynasty (hMC). Hatti is early in the reign of Mursili I (c. 1620–1590, hMC); his accession date is approximate, so the world names him only with "c.". | Chad 2026-11-18 (chose "1615 BCE") | It falls safely inside every high-chronology estimate, and it shows the town fully rebuilt with its paintings fresh. The T7 check reads the decades-long interval as running from the first earthquake and the 20–90 days from the final one, so the living town lasts until c. 1612 (`research/verification/T7.md`). |
| D17 | **The size budget for `index.html` stays at 256 kB** (closes O6; confirms D7's provisional figure). The first real build, after work order 001, is 132 kB: about 71 kB of terrain data and about 61 kB of engine. A work order that cannot fit the budget at a quality worth showing stops and reports the numbers; it does not raise the budget (D11). | Chad 2026-11-18 ("O6, size budget looks fine") | The island used half the budget, which leaves about 124 kB for the town, plants and people. If that proves too little, D11 already says how the law changes. |
| D18 | **The excavated part of the town follows the excavated plan, and the edge of the evidence is recorded and shown.** The streets, squares and buildings that have been dug stand in the world where they were found, drawn from a plan whose licence allows it. Outside the limit of the excavation the town is reconstruction: built by the same rules, recorded as a liberty, and marked so that a viewer can tell the two apart. How large the whole town is drawn rests on research (lane T2b), not on a default. | Chad 2026-11-18 ("we have archaeological evidence for the street plan of Akrotiri"; "may need to determine what the edges of the data for this are so we can note it"; "Let's do some research to base it on the available archaeological and historical data") | The first draft of work order 002 would have invented the whole street plan, because the excavators' own plans are copyright. The plan is evidence all the same, and an openly licensed drawing of it exists. Saying where the evidence stops is what keeps the rest honest. |
| D19 | **The town is built whole and thriving, from the outside first.** Work order 002 builds streets and buildings as seen from outside, with no interiors, wall paintings, people, animals, plants or ships yet. Nothing of the last weeks is shown: no earthquake damage, rubble or repairs (D15, D16). The harbour waits for a later order. | Chad 2026-11-18 ("I want this to be all before the eruption. The idea is to show the world thriving before the disaster."; "We'll deal with harbour questions later.") | The point of the world is the life before the disaster. The harbour has not been found, and two candidate places stay open (T1c). |
| D20 | **Views are added, never replaced** (amends D10). The views check photographs keys 1 to N, where `--views N` is from 1 to 9 and defaults to 6. It fails if the page's menu lists a different number of viewpoints, or if a key does not change the picture. Work order 002 keeps the six island views and adds three of the town. | Chad 2026-11-18 ("Let's add more views, not remove the ones we have.") | The six island views guard the terrain, sea and light against regressions while the town is built. |
| D21 | **The two open licence questions go to the licensor in writing, with a change of land data as the fallback.** Chad asks the Copernicus help desk whether an edited reconstruction still counts as the licensed data, and whether accepting the licence needs any step beyond downloading. If no usable answer comes, the land data can be rebuilt from NASA's NASADEM, once its terms are read and recorded. Until then the project treats the data as covered and carries every notice. | Chad 2026-11-18 ("draft me a message for them about our license questions and we'll keep option 2 as backup") | No lawyer is available. A written answer from the licensor is the best evidence short of legal advice, and the terrain tools make a change of dataset a rebuild, not a rewrite. |
