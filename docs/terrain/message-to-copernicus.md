# A message to Copernicus about the elevation licence: draft, not sent

Written on 2026-11-18 for D21. The message drafted in Chad's earlier session was never saved to the repository, so this is a fresh draft. **Nothing has been sent.** This is research, not legal advice (`docs/research/verification/T1c.md`, "Licence verdict").

## Why it is worth five minutes, and no more

- The licence already allows what the project does: adapting the data and publishing the result, free of charge, with no non-commercial condition (T1c#40, T1c#41). The project is free and not for sale (D27), which lowers the stakes further but does not change the licence.
- On the first question the project already takes the cautious reading: it treats the edited terrain as covered and carries every notice the licence asks for.
- So the message asks for confirmation, not permission. If no answer comes in a month, D21's fallback stands and nothing changes.

## Where to send it

Found by a web search and one page fetch on 2026-11-18. **The form itself could not be opened by the fetch (HTTP 403), so check it in a browser.**

- If that form needs an account you do not want to make: the contact form at <https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/about#contact-form>, or the community forum at <https://forum.dataspace.copernicus.eu/>, where the support team has answered a question about citing this dataset.

## One thing found on the way

A Copernicus Data Space notice dated 17 July 2026 says that from 28 July 2026 the **view service** for the 30 m model is restricted to registered users, who tick a box in their profile. It names only the view service. It says nothing about downloads, about data already downloaded, or about the copy on the Amazon open-data bucket, which is where this project's tiles came from without an account (T1c#44). It was read once, by a fetch, and is not a checked claim. It is the reason for the second question below.

Notice: <https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/node/4044>

## The draft

> **Subject:** Two questions on the licence for Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (COP-DEM-GLO-30-F) in an edited public reconstruction
>
> Hello,
>
> I am building a free, non-commercial reconstruction of the island of Santorini as it may have looked in the Bronze Age, before its eruption. It runs in a web browser and its source is public: [link].
>
> The land heights start from Copernicus DEM GLO-30 tiles, downloaded in 2026 from the public Amazon open-data bucket. I then changed them heavily: resampled to about 40 m, cropped to the island, and edited to undo the eruption (land restored, deposits removed, sea level changed). The page and the repository carry the notice your licence asks for, "produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018 provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved", and the no-liability sentence.
>
> May I ask two things?
>
> 1. Does data changed this much still count as the Copernicus WorldDEM-30 under the licence, so that the notice and conditions of Article 6 are the right ones to carry? I have assumed that it does.
> 2. Does accepting the licence need any step beyond downloading the tiles, for example registering an account? I ask because of your notice of 17 July 2026 about the view service.
>
> If I should credit the data differently, please tell me how and I will change it.
>
> Thank you,
> [name]
